What nobody can promise

What's moving right now

The trend report.

Our read on formats, platforms, creators, advertising, search, AI and consumer behaviour — as of 26 September 2026. Trends move fast: this is analysis, not a live data feed.

Formats78/100

Silent-first editing

Captions carry the hook while sound does the texture. Assume the first two seconds are muted and the viewer is in public.

Platforms84/100

Search inside social

Product research increasingly starts in TikTok and Instagram search. Captions, on-screen text and covers now do keyword work.

Creators71/100

Small roster, deep relationships

Brands consolidating from fifty one-off creators to eight recurring ones, with usage rights and whitelisting built in from the start.

Advertising88/100

Creative as the targeting layer

Broad audiences plus high creative variance beats granular targeting on most consolidated accounts.

Search80/100

Answer engines as a discovery surface

Model-generated answers summarise categories before a click happens. Entity clarity and citations matter more than keyword density.

AI76/100

Human-in-the-loop production

AI for volume and speed; humans for taste, claims and brand voice. The winning workflow is reviewed, not automated end to end.

Consumer69/100

Skepticism as default

Audiences assume production is paid until proven otherwise. Specificity, mechanism and visible trade-offs outperform superlatives.

E-commerce82/100

Margin-first scaling

Teams moving from ROAS screenshots to contribution margin after returns and shipping as the decision metric.
