Engagement models

Structure follows the outcome.

Most partners start on a retainer or a productized program, then expand. Performance-aligned structures require clean measurement on both sides — we'll tell you honestly whether you have it.

01

Retainer

Ongoing growth partnership

A monthly team working as your growth function — strategy, creative, media and measurement in one cadence.

  • Dedicated team & weekly rhythm
  • Quarterly planning
  • Full system ownership
02

Productized

Defined packages for specific outcomes

Fixed scope, fixed rhythm, clear deliverables. Best when you know exactly which gap you're filling.

  • Clear scope & timeline
  • Predictable investment
  • Fast to start
03

Project

Campaigns, launches, websites, branding

A defined build with a start and an end: a launch, a site, a brand system, a creative library.

  • Milestone-based
  • Defined deliverables
  • Handover documentation
04

Fractional CMO

Strategic leadership, part-time

Senior marketing leadership without a full-time executive hire — planning, hiring support and team accountability.

  • Exec-level strategy
  • Team & vendor oversight
  • Board-ready reporting
05

Performance

Aligned compensation

Where the data, tracking and margin model support it, structures tied to agreed performance metrics.

  • Shared upside
  • Agreed metrics & baselines
  • Requires clean measurement
