Overview

An anonymized engagement pattern: local search, short-form video and platform hygiene across nine locations.

Type: Anonymized example. Industry: Hospitality / Restaurants. Year: 2026.

02

The challenge

A restaurant group with nine locations had nine inconsistent Google Business Profiles, three unverified, two with duplicate listings, and an Instagram presence run by whoever at each site had time. Discovery was accidental. Reviews went unanswered for weeks.

03

The insight

For multi-location hospitality, the highest-leverage marketing surface isn't the feed — it's the moment someone searches 'near me' and compares three profiles in eight seconds. Photos, hours, response rate and one memorable dish do more than a month of posts.

04

The idea

Treat each location as its own local business with shared creative infrastructure: one content system feeding nine profiles, standardised photography, a review response playbook, and a central calendar with local slots.

05

Creative direction

Dish-led vertical video shot in batches — one shoot day per quarter per location, cut into a month of posts. A recurring 'what's on tonight' format. Menu photography rebuilt to a consistent spec so profiles look like one brand.

06

Campaign execution

Profile audit and cleanup first: duplicates resolved, verification completed where possible, categories, service areas, hours and attributes standardised. Then the content system, then paid local reach around high-intent windows.

07

Distribution

Google Business Profile as the primary surface, organic social for locals and staff advocacy, geo-fenced paid social around peak booking windows, email for events and private hire.

08

Testing

Profile photo order, primary category, post cadence, and offer framing per location. Paid tests on radius, daypart and creative format.

Measurement

Profile actions — calls, direction requests, bookings — by location, review velocity and average rating, plus paid cost per booking where trackable.

Learnings

Local growth is mostly operational hygiene executed consistently. The creative matters, but a verified, complete, well-photographed profile with fast review responses is the compounding asset.
