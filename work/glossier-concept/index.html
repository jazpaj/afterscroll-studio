Overview

A concept campaign that turns the group chat — the real beauty decision engine — into a content format.

Type: Concept campaign. Industry: Beauty. Year: 2026.

02

The challenge

Beauty brands built on community eventually hit a wall: the community is treated as a marketing asset rather than a product input, and the content becomes polished enough to feel like advertising again. Meanwhile the actual purchase decision happens in a screenshot sent to three friends.

03

The insight

Nobody buys beauty alone. The decisive moment is a photo sent to a group chat with the caption 'thoughts?'. That exchange — blunt, funny, specific — is more persuasive than any campaign film, and brands are absent from it.

04

The idea

SECOND OPINION — a format where every product story is told as a conversation between three people who disagree. Real texts, real hesitation, real verdicts. The brand's role is to host the argument, not to win it.

05

Creative direction

Split-screen vertical video, typed-message overlays, no voiceover. Ugly-good lighting. A recurring on-screen device: the verdict card at the end, sometimes negative — which is exactly what makes the positive ones land.

Layout mockups indicating format and structure — not finished assets.

06

Campaign execution

A rolling call for real group chats, cleared and anonymised with permission. Community members become recurring characters. A monthly 'verdicts' recap on email and SMS closes the loop and feeds the next round.

07

Distribution

Organic on brand and community accounts, paid amplification on the highest hold-rate cuts, SMS as the fast lane for drops, email for the editorial recap.

08

Testing

Verdict-first vs question-first opens. Three-person vs two-person casts. Product-in-frame at 0s vs 6s. Offer tests on bundle vs single.

Learnings
