How we work

Four moving parts.

Recovery and appeal support, verification, tracking and clean platform infrastructure.

01

Triage

We document what happened, what the platform says, and what evidence exists before anything is submitted.

02

Preparation

Identity documents, business records, domain control and policy-aligned narratives assembled properly.

03

Process management

Submissions, follow-ups, escalation paths and support threads tracked to a conclusion.

04

Hardening

Once you're back: permissions, 2FA, admin redundancy, verification and monitoring so it doesn't repeat.
