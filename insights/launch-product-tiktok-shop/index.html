How To Launch A Product On TikTok Shop

A sequenced launch plan: setup, seeding, content, affiliates and amplification — in the order that avoids cancelled orders.

By Afterscroll Strategy Desk01 May 20268 min readE-commerce

A shop launch fails in operations far more often than in creative. Stock sync, variant naming, fulfilment speed and policy compliance decide whether momentum turns into revenue or into refunds and a damaged account standing.

Four weeks out — infrastructure

  • Shop setup, business verification and payout details completed and confirmed.
  • Catalogue built with accurate variants, clean titles and compliant claims.
  • Stock sync tested with a real order end to end.
  • Fulfilment and returns process documented with an owner and an SLA.

Three weeks out — seeding

Get product into the hands of creators before launch, with a brief but genuine creative freedom. Recruit more than you need; response rates are always lower than planned. Confirm rights and disclosure requirements in writing at this stage, not later.

Two weeks out — content bank

  1. Build 15–25 assets before launch day: demonstrations, objection answers, price-clarity cuts, unboxing.
  2. Write five hook variants per format so you can react quickly.
  3. Prepare the live-shopping script if you're running one, with a rehearsal.
  4. Line up customer service coverage for launch week.

Launch week

Publish frequently, reply to every comment, and watch which format earns hold rate. Amplify the best organic content with paid rather than launching cold ads. Keep a daily eye on order cancellation and fulfilment metrics — they gate your visibility.

After

Within two weeks you'll know your winning format and your two best affiliates. Consolidate spend and attention there, and convert launch buyers into an owned list immediately, because platform audiences are rented.

The most common mistake

Launching before fulfilment is proven. One wave of cancellations early can suppress visibility for weeks.
