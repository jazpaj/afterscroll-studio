Search inside social

Product research increasingly starts in TikTok and Instagram search. Captions, on-screen text and covers now do keyword work.

Creators71/100

Small roster, deep relationships

Advertising88/100

Creative as the targeting layer

Broad audiences plus high creative variance beats granular targeting on most consolidated accounts.

Search80/100

Answer engines as a discovery surface

Model-generated answers summarise categories before a click happens. Entity clarity and citations matter more than keyword density.

AI76/100

Human-in-the-loop production

AI for volume and speed; humans for taste, claims and brand voice. The winning workflow is reviewed, not automated end to end.

Consumer69/100

Skepticism as default
