How To Optimize Content For AI Search

A concrete editing checklist for making pages that answer engines can quote correctly.

By Afterscroll Search Desk31 July 20267 min readSearch & GEO

Models are lazy readers with excellent recall. They reward content that states its answer plainly, near the top, in language that survives being extracted from context. Most existing marketing content fails on all three counts.

Structure

  1. Answer the page's core question in the first 60 words, plainly, without a preamble about how important the topic is.
  2. Use descriptive headings that read as questions or claims, not as clever labels.
  3. Keep one idea per section, with the conclusion first and the reasoning after.
  4. Include a short definition block for any term you want to be quoted on.

Language

  • Prefer specific numbers over ranges, and ranges over adjectives.
  • Name the conditions under which a claim holds. Conditional accuracy makes you safer to cite.
  • Avoid pronouns spanning paragraphs — extracted sentences lose their referents.
  • Write the trade-offs. Balanced content gets cited in comparison answers.

Technical support

  • Structured data for organisation, product, article, FAQ and local business where applicable.
  • Stable URLs and clean canonicals so citations don't rot.
  • Server-rendered content — if the answer only exists after client-side hydration, assume it may be missed.
  • An accurate, crawlable about and contact page. Entity signals start there.

What to track

A fixed prompt set, checked monthly: is the brand mentioned, is the description accurate, which URL or source is credited. Add branded search volume and direct traffic as the commercial proxy. Don't promise rankings you can't observe.

One editing pass that helps most

Take any existing article and move the answer to the top. That single change improves snippet eligibility, reader satisfaction and extraction quality at once.
